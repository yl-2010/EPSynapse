About

EPSynapse is a web app and an iOS app that I built with a team of 3 other students at the Frontier Cascadia Hackathon. It is a synthesized dashboard for students, combining Canvas, Microsoft Outlook, OneNote, OneDrive, and many other common apps that students use.

Students' learning materials are often fragmented between all of those different apps, so EPSynapse puts everything into one unified dashboard for students to see, with an agentic layer built into the backend. Students can use the built-in agent to help study for tests, learn material, get feedback on their work, and anything else they ask the agent to do.

The agent has direct access to Canvas through the students' API keys, and access to all Microsoft apps through Microsoft Entra ID, while still having built-in safeguards preventing students from using it to directly cheat.

We built the first version in under 12 hours at the Frontier Cascadia Hackathon, winning third place and $1,000. Now we're working with my school to get it integrated directly into the school's Microsoft and class scheduling systems.

I'm the leading member of the team, with the most development experience, so I do most of the actual technical development myself, with the rest of the team helping with graphic design, UX design, and finding and fixing bugs.

- Yan Levin
