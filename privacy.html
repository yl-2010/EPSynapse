Privacy

EPSynapse is a student app. Home is epsynapse.com.

Google sign-in

When you sign in with Google we keep your name, email, and profile photo so you stay logged in. That is all we take from Google.

School and student ID

Settings let you type a school and a student ID. Both are optional. They stay on your account so a school admin can match you to a roster later.

Canvas, OneDrive, Outlook

Tokens you connect stay on the Mac API behind api.epsynapse.com. The browser does not keep those keys.

We do not sell this

No ads, no resale, no handing your roster to a third party. Questions go to the team that runs epsynapse.com.
