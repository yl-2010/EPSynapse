Privacy

EPSynapse is a student home page. Home is epsynapse.com. This page lists what the app stores about you, where it lives, and how to remove it.

Your account

When you sign in with Google we keep the Google account id, your name, email, and profile picture. That is what keeps you signed in and puts your name in Settings.

Eastside Prep students will later sign in with the school Microsoft account instead. For those accounts we will keep the school Microsoft identity and the Microsoft Graph tokens that let the app read OneDrive, OneNote, Outlook, and Teams. That sign-in is not live yet.

Connections and keys

For Canvas we keep the access token you paste in Settings, or for EPS students the OAuth tokens from the school's Canvas. The app uses it to read your courses, assignments, and grades and to mark todos done.

For chat we keep the model API keys you paste for Groq, Gemini, or OpenRouter. Chat and note classification run on the provider you picked, using your own key. Each request goes straight from the EPSynapse server to that provider. EPSynapse has no key of its own in the loop.

All of these secrets are encrypted at rest with a server-side key. The browser never keeps them.

Your school work

Uploaded schedule PDFs and the classes parsed from them, including period labels, teachers, rooms, and meeting times. Notes you paste and the class the model assigned them to. Todos and their done state. Files you attach to a class or a todo. Chat history with the assistant.

Where it lives

Everything sits on the EPSynapse server behind api.epsynapse.com. Today that is a Mac in Washington state. It is moving to a Google Cloud project owned by the school so the data outlives the students who built this.

What we do not do

Nothing is shared between students. Your notes, files, and chats are visible to your account only. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads. Model requests go only to the provider you chose, with your key.

Delete everything

Settings has a Delete account control under Sign out. Paused accounts have the same control on their sign-in card. It removes your account, connections, keys, PDFs, classes, notes, todos, files, and chats right away. There is no waiting period and nothing is kept.

Questions

Ask the team that runs epsynapse.com. A contact address will appear here once one is set up.
