Privacy

EPSynapse is a student app for school pulses and a personal dashboard.

When you sign in with Google we store your Google name, email, and profile photo so we can keep you logged in. School and student ID that you type in settings stay on your account so a school admin can match you to a roster later.

Canvas, OneDrive, and Outlook tokens you connect stay on our Mac API. We do not sell this data. Questions: email the team that runs epsynapse.com.
