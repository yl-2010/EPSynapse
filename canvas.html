Canvas token

Classes, homework, and grades come from Canvas. Chat does not take a token. Make one in Canvas, then save it in Settings.

Log into Canvas

  1. Open eastsideprep.instructure.com. Another school? Use that school's Canvas URL instead.
  2. Eastside Prep sends you to four11. Tap @eastsideprep.org login. That is Microsoft with your school email. Do not tap Parent/Guardian Login. Do not tap Old Canvas Login Page.
  3. Finish the Microsoft sign-in. You should land on your Canvas dashboard, with classes on the left. Stay on that tab. You need it open.

Find New Access Token

  1. On the left, click Account. That is your picture or the Account label at the bottom of the global nav. A small tray slides out.
  2. Click Settings. Not Notifications. Not Profile. Settings.
  3. Scroll down the page. Past your picture, past the contact info, until the heading Approved Integrations.
  4. Click Add New Access Token. Some Canvas sites label this + New Access Token. Same button.

If that button is greyed out, the school turned student tokens off. Ask a Canvas admin. Do not open Developer Keys. That is a different, admin-only page.

Purpose, expiration, permissions

There is no permissions list. No scopes. No checkboxes. Do not look for Read, Write, or Courses. A Canvas token can do what you can do. EPSynapse only reads courses, assignments, planner items, and grades.

  1. Purpose. Type EPSynapse.
  2. Expiration. Students have to pick a date. Canvas often caps this at about 30 days. Click the calendar, pick the last day it allows. Leave the time as it is, or 11:59 PM.
  3. Staff can sometimes leave expiration empty. If the field has a star, pick a date.
  4. Click Generate Token.
  5. Copy the token now. It is a long string. It does not start with gsk_. Canvas shows the full value once. If you close the box without copying, make a new token.

Where to paste it

  1. Come back to epsynapse.com and sign in with Google.
  2. Tap the gear in the bottom-left corner.
  3. Tap Canvas.
  4. Leave Canvas URL as https://eastsideprep.instructure.com unless you use another school. If you do, paste that host.
  5. Paste the token in Access token.
  6. Tap Save. That is the gold Save on the Canvas pane.
  7. You should land back on the settings list. Canvas must say Connected, not "URL and token".
  8. Close settings. Classes and homework should load. Chat can use them too.

Where the token lives

The token is stored on your Google account, on the Mac API. It is not in the public site files, and it is not read from the chat box.

If it expires or you lose it, make a new one in the same Approved Integrations section. In EPSynapse, tap Replace token, then Save.
